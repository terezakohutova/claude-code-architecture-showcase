# Inventory - reference (stable facts)

Facts about logged items that don't change once recorded. Current listing
status lives in [inventory-status.md](inventory-status.md), not here.

## Pricing bands (by category, condition-adjusted)

| Category | Good condition | Excellent condition | Mint/unused |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glassware | 15-35 | 30-60 | 55-120 |
| Ceramics | 20-45 | 40-90 | 80-180 |
| Textiles | 10-25 | 20-50 | 45-100 |
| Small furniture | 40-120 | 100-250 | n/a (rarely mint) |

Adjust up for a documented maker/maker's mark, down for undocumented
repairs beyond minor wear.

## House condition grading (used consistently across all listings)

- **Good** - honest age-appropriate wear, fully usable, no active damage.
- **Excellent** - light wear only, no repairs, all original parts.
- **Mint/unused** - no signs of use at all.
