# Voice examples (published copy)

What the shop has actually posted, copied here verbatim when it went out,
so `brand-voice-reviewer` compares drafts against real published copy and
not against a style guide. Add a new example when a listing or reply goes
live and reads the way the shop wants to sound; remove one that no longer
does. (Fictional shop, like everything in this repo.)

Notes on what these have in common, for a reviewer who wants the short
version: facts first, one sentence per fact, flaws named plainly with
where they are, price justified in one line by something checkable, no
adjectives about how good the item is.
